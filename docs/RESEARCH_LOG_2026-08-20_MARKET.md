# StockForge Research Log — Marketplace Differentiation

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Purpose:** establish a permanent rule that StockForge research must include real marketplace conditions, not only marketplace submission rules.

## Findings

1. Broad construction imagery is extremely saturated on Adobe Stock. Current result pages showed 49M+ results for `construction`, 3.2M+ for `project management`, and 546K+ for `construction tech project management`.
2. Shutterstock's live Trends dashboard exposes demand/growth signals for narrower and emerging queries. This supports using trend signals as an opportunity input, while recognizing that platform-specific trend metrics are not equivalent to sales.
3. Adobe's 2026 Creative Trends emphasize relevance, usefulness, human connection, authenticity, and regional specificity. This supports a strategy based on specific commercial stories rather than generic AI spectacle.
4. Shutterstock's 2026 content-brief guidance emphasizes focused collections, clear customer demand, distinct human stories, and strong metadata.
5. Shutterstock's 2026 AI-production guidance distinguishes interesting AI images from campaign-ready creative. StockForge should therefore optimize for usable commercial creative, not prompt novelty.

## Strategic change

The Market Intelligence layer is now a required upstream input to the production pipeline:

```text
Marketplace evidence
    ↓
Demand / saturation hypothesis
    ↓
Buyer use case
    ↓
Commercial visual problem
    ↓
Differentiated concept
    ↓
Composition / copy-space plan
    ↓
Prompt
    ↓
Generation
```

## Benchmark implication

The first benchmark, construction professionals reviewing architectural plans, demonstrates that a technically good image can still be commercially generic. The next benchmark family should test narrower intersections such as construction + AI safety, construction + digital workflow, construction + climate resilience, construction + remote inspection, or other topics only after current marketplace evidence validates the opportunity.
