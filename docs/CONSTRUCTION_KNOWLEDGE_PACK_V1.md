# Construction Knowledge Pack v1

This pack defines initial reality-aware workflows for StockForge. It is a structured research target, not permission to invent technical procedures.

## 01 — Wall moisture inspection

- Task: investigate suspected moisture in a building material.
- Typical targets: drywall, plaster, wood, masonry.
- Tool: moisture meter appropriate to the material and inspection method.
- Affordance: instrument interacts with the material surface according to its design.
- Evidence: suspected damp/stained area, inspector positioning, meter interaction, observation of reading.
- Buyer jobs: building inspection, property maintenance, restoration, insurance documentation.
- Must not: meter floating, unrelated target, arbitrary pose.

## 02 — Room dimensional measurement

- Task: document room dimensions.
- Tool: laser distance meter or appropriate dimensional tool.
- Target: opposite wall or other defined measurement point.
- Affordance: device aimed from a plausible measurement origin to the intended target.
- Evidence: room geometry makes the measurement understandable.
- Buyer jobs: construction, renovation, interior design, architectural documentation.
- Must not: random laser direction, device aimed at a person, tool used like a surveying instrument it is not.

## 03 — Door/window opening measurement

- Task: verify opening dimensions before fabrication, installation, renovation, or documentation.
- Tool: laser distance meter, tape measure, or appropriate dimensional tool.
- Target: jamb-to-jamb, sill-to-head, or other relevant opening dimension.
- Evidence: tool spans or targets the actual opening.
- Buyer jobs: contractor, installer, architect, renovation company.
- Must not: tool disconnected from the opening being measured.

## 04 — Concrete crack inspection

- Task: inspect/document a visible crack in concrete.
- Tools: crack gauge, ruler, flashlight, camera, or other appropriate inspection aid.
- Target: actual concrete crack.
- Evidence: inspection aid aligned with the crack; crack visible and physically plausible.
- Buyer jobs: QA/QC, structural inspection, maintenance, engineering consultancy.
- Must not: invented crack floating away from the surface or tool unrelated to defect.

## 05 — Rebar scanning

- Task: locate reinforcement inside concrete before drilling/cutting or for documentation.
- Tool: rebar/cover scanner appropriate to concrete scanning.
- Target: concrete surface containing embedded reinforcement.
- Affordance: scanner moved across/against the concrete surface according to its design.
- Evidence: structural element, scanner position, plausible scan path.
- Buyer jobs: contractor, structural engineer, renovation, drilling/cutting safety.
- Must not: scanner held in air or used as a generic metal detector without context.

## 06 — Concrete cover measurement

- Task: estimate reinforcement cover depth.
- Tool: appropriate cover meter/rebar locator.
- Target: reinforced concrete surface.
- Affordance: instrument positioned/scanned on concrete.
- Evidence: concrete element and measurement workflow are visible.
- Buyer jobs: structural inspection, QA/QC, engineering documentation.
- Must not: imply exposed reinforcement is being measured by a cover meter unless the scene explains the task.

## 07 — Thermal inspection

- Task: investigate thermal anomalies in a building or equipment context.
- Tool: thermal imaging camera or appropriate IR instrument.
- Target: wall, ceiling, electrical component, HVAC component, pipe, or other justified target.
- Affordance: camera aimed at the target from a plausible inspection position.
- Evidence: inspection context and target relationship.
- Buyer jobs: building inspection, energy audit, electrical maintenance, HVAC diagnostics.
- Must not: arbitrary camera direction or decorative thermal graphics unrelated to an inspection target.

## 08 — Floor level checking

- Task: verify level/elevation of a floor or construction element.
- Tool: laser level or appropriate leveling instrument.
- Target: physical reference surface/line.
- Affordance: instrument positioned to establish a level reference.
- Evidence: physical room/structure and plausible reference relationship.
- Buyer jobs: contractor, flooring installer, surveyor, QA/QC.
- Must not: confuse laser level with distance measurement or create arbitrary glowing geometry.

## 09 — Concrete surface inspection

- Task: visually inspect concrete condition, finish, defects, or workmanship.
- Tools: flashlight, straightedge, ruler, camera, inspection tools appropriate to the specific task.
- Target: actual concrete surface/detail.
- Evidence: inspector attention directed at the surface.
- Buyer jobs: contractor, QA/QC, engineering, maintenance.
- Must not: add defects that are unrelated to the stated inspection purpose.

## 10 — Construction safety inspection

- Task: inspect site conditions for safety/compliance issues.
- Tools: checklist, clipboard/tablet, camera, measuring or observation tools as appropriate.
- Targets: access routes, PPE, barriers, housekeeping, temporary works, equipment, signage, work areas.
- Evidence: inspector visibly evaluating a real site condition.
- Buyer jobs: contractor, site manager, safety consultant, developer, insurer.
- Must not: create dangerous behavior merely to make the image look dramatic.

## Research rule

Each workflow must be upgraded with authoritative references before being treated as a detailed technical procedure. Manufacturer documentation establishes tool behavior; standards and professional sources establish practice; marketplace research establishes commercial framing.
